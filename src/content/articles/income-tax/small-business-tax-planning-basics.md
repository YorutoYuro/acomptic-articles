Proactive tax planning shouldn't happen at the very last week of the financial year. For small business owners and independent operators in India, structuring daily expenses correctly can make a substantial difference in taxable income.

## Claiming Legitimate Business Expenses

Every expense directly incurred for the operation of your business—ranging from software subscriptions and office utilities to travel and inventory restocking—can generally be claimed as a deduction against business revenue.

## Maintaining Clean Ledger Books

To support any deduction claimed during assessment, having auditable records is non-negotiable. Ensure that your transaction records map accurately to your bank statements and invoices.

* Separate personal and business expenditures completely.
* Keep digital copies of high-value equipment or inventory invoices.
* Review ledger summaries quarterly rather than annually.

By staying organized throughout the year, filing returns becomes a predictable routine rather than a stressful scramble.
