Business records may contain customer names, phone numbers, purchase details, and supplier information.

## Know Where Your Data Lives

Understand whether records are stored locally or in the cloud.

## Use Basic Security Practices

Use strong passwords, limited permissions, and regular backups.

> Good data protection starts with knowing what you store and who can reach it.
