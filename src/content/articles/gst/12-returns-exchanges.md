Returns are normal in retail, but informal handling can create confusing records.

## Link Returns to Original Sales

Identify the original bill and returned products whenever possible.

## Separate Refunds From Exchanges

Record payment changes and stock movements clearly.

> A return is not just a negative sale; it is a stock and payment event.
