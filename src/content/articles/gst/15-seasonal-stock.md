Festivals, school seasons, and weather changes can shift demand quickly.

## Learn From Previous Seasons

Review comparable sales periods and identify changing demand.

## Include Supplier Lead Time

Consider delivery time and storage limits before ordering.

> Good seasonal planning balances expected demand with the cost of holding stock.
