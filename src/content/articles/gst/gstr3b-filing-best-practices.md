Filing your monthly GSTR-3B return correctly is essential to maintaining a clean compliance record and protecting your working capital. However, minor input discrepancies during data entry can often trigger notices or mismatches with G2B portals.

## 1. Reconciling Input Tax Credit (ITC)

One of the most frequent errors businesses make is claiming Input Tax Credit without verifying matching entries in their purchase registers. Always cross-check your invoices against your suppliers' filed returns to ensure full eligibility.

## 2. Timing Your Outward Supplies

Ensure that all B2B sales invoices generated during the tax period are accounted for properly. Missing out on reported turnover can lead to backdated interest charges and compounding penalties.
