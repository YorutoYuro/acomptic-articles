Tax-related work becomes harder when invoices are incomplete or records are scattered.

## Record Required Details

Maintain appropriate invoice numbers, dates, quantities, and tax information.

## Keep Supporting Documents

Retain supplier invoices and review records periodically. Confirm applicable obligations with a qualified professional.

> Good preparation for compliance begins with good everyday records.
