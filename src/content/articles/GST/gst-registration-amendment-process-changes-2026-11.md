If you have ever waited days for an officer to approve a small change in your GST registration, this update will come as good news. Under Notification No. 05/2026-Central Tax (26 October 2026), Rule 21 of the CGST Rules has been replaced, and the GST portal already works the new way.

The short version: most amendments now go through on their own, but there is a new verification step you have to finish on time.

## What Stays the Same

If you are changing your Principal Place of Business, nothing is different. A tax officer will still review the application under Rule 19, so expect the usual wait.

## What Is Now Instant

Almost every other change no longer needs an officer's approval. Your registration certificate is updated as soon as you submit the application and the portal validates it. This covers things like:

- Changing the legal name of the business
- Adding, removing or retiring partners, directors, Karta and similar persons
- Adding an additional place of business
- Updating any other detail from your original registration form, except the principal place of business

## The New Catch: OTP Verification

Faster approval comes with a safety check. The mobile number and email address of two people must be verified with OTP:

- The primary authorised signatory
- One promoter, partner, director, Karta, member or trustee of your choice

You can do this on the GST portal under **Services → Registration → OTP verification of GSTIN**.

You have 3 days after submitting the application to finish it. If you miss that window, the application will not get an ARN, which means the amendment simply does not happen and you will have to apply again.

## Automatic Checks on PAN, DIN and CIN

The portal will also check your PAN, and your DIN and CIN where they apply, by itself. Once these checks pass, your ARN is generated and the amended certificate becomes available for download on the portal.

## Before You Apply

A little preparation avoids most problems:

- Make sure the phones and email inboxes of the people who will receive the OTPs are working and within reach
- Keep your PAN, DIN and CIN details ready and matching your records
- Fill in FORM GST REG-14 carefully and upload the right supporting documents
- Complete the OTP step the same day, so the 3-day limit never becomes an issue

> When changes go live without an officer reviewing them, a typo goes live too, so double-check everything before you hit submit.
