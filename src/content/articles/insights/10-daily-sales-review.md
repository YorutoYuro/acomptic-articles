Closing a shop is already busy. A daily review should be short, repeatable, and useful.

## Check Four Things

Review total bills, collections, credit sales, and returns.

## Resolve Differences Early

Investigate missing entries while transactions are easy to recall.

> Five careful minutes today can save an hour of searching later.
