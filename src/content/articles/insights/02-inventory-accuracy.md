Inventory problems often begin with small oversights: a sale that was not recorded, a purchase entered twice, or a damaged item left in the available quantity.

## Record Every Stock Movement

Record purchases, sales, returns, and adjustments as they happen.

## Review Important Products

Fast-moving and high-value goods deserve more frequent checks.

> Accurate stock begins with consistent records, not complicated reports.
