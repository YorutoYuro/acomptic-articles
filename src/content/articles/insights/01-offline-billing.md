A shop cannot pause every time the internet connection drops. Customers still need bills, stock still needs updating, and sales still need recording.

## Keep the Counter Moving

Offline-capable software allows routine work to continue without waiting for a server response.

## Sync Without Interrupting Work

A practical offline-first system stores records safely and synchronizes them when a connection becomes available.

> A dependable billing system should work when the shop is open, not only when the internet is.
