Sales bills show what leaves the shop, while purchase entries explain what comes in.

## Capture Details at Arrival

Record suppliers, invoice dates, quantities, and purchase rates.

## Keep Corrections Traceable

Record returns and adjustments clearly.

> Stock accuracy depends on recording both sides of the movement.
