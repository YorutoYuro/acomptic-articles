A slow checkout creates queues and interrupts the shopkeeper's attention.

## Remove Repeated Work

Product search, quantity entry, discounts, and payment recording should follow a natural sequence.

## Keep Bills Understandable

Customers should clearly see items, quantities, prices, and the final amount.

> The best checkout flow is the one that feels almost invisible.
