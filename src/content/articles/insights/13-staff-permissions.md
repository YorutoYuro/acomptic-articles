Not every employee needs access to every software setting.

## Match Permissions to Responsibilities

Cashiers may need billing access without permission to change sensitive settings.

## Keep Activity Traceable

Use individual staff accounts instead of shared passwords.

> Convenient access should not mean unlimited access.
