Reporting screens can contain dozens of charts, but small businesses need practical answers.

## Start With Useful Summaries

Review daily sales, collections, low-stock products, and customer balances.

## Ask Better Questions

Use reports to investigate changes and plan the next step.

> A useful report is one that leads to a clearer next step.
