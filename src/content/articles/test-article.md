# Introduction

Input Tax Credit (ITC) allows business owners to offset the tax they pay on purchases against their output tax liability.

## Key Rules

1. You must hold a valid tax invoice or debit note.
2. The goods or services must have been received.
3. The supplier must have filed their GST return and deposited the tax.
